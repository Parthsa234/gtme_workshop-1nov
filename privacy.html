Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you visit this site or book the Live Build Workshop, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • When you book: your name, email, phone number and payment status, passed to us by Razorpay.
  • When you attend: your name as shown in Zoom and any questions you ask in the session.
  • When you browse: standard technical data such as pages viewed, device and browser type, and the ad or link that brought you here, through analytics and advertising tools such as Google and Meta.

Why we use it

  • To send your Zoom link, reminders, templates and replay.
  • To answer your questions and handle refunds.
  • To tell you about later sessions and programmes. You can opt out of these at any time.
  • To measure which ads and pages work.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the workshop: Razorpay for payments, Zoom for the session, our email and WhatsApp providers for messages, and analytics and advertising platforms for measurement. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

Booking and invoice records are kept as long as Indian tax law requires. Marketing contact details are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop marketing messages, by writing to hello@gtme.school. We reply within seven working days.

Cookies

The site uses cookies and similar tools for analytics and ad measurement. You can block them in your browser settings; the site still works.
